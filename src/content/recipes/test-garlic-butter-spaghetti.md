## Ingredients

- `200g` spaghetti
- `3 tbsp` butter
- `3` garlic cloves, finely minced
- `1 handful` fresh parsley, chopped
- `30g` parmesan, finely grated
- to taste salt and black pepper

## Steps

1. Bring a large pot of well-salted water to a boil and cook the spaghetti until al dente.
2. Meanwhile, melt the butter in a large pan over medium-low heat, add the garlic and cook 1 minute until fragrant (don't brown it).
3. Reserve a splash of pasta water, drain the spaghetti, and toss it in the garlic butter with a little pasta water to emulsify.
4. Off the heat, stir through the parmesan and parsley, season with salt and pepper, and serve immediately.
