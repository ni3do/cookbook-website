## Ingredients

- `700g` boneless skinless chicken breasts (or thighs), pounded to ~1.5–2 cm even thickness, or cut into tenders
- `80g` all-purpose flour
- `2` large eggs, beaten
- `120g` panko breadcrumbs
- `1 tsp` fine salt
- `1 tsp` sweet paprika
- `1/2 tsp` garlic powder
- `1/2 tsp` black pepper
- Oil spray (olive or neutral)
- Lemon wedges, to serve

## Optional in the crumbs

- `20g` Parmesan, finely grated
- `1/2 tsp` dried oregano

## Steps

1. Pat the chicken dry. Season lightly with a pinch of salt on both sides.

2. Set up three shallow bowls: (1) flour mixed with salt, paprika, garlic powder, and pepper; (2) beaten eggs; (3) panko mixed with optional Parmesan and oregano.

3. Dredge each piece in flour, shake off excess, dip in egg, then press firmly into panko so both sides are well coated. Rest breaded pieces on a rack or plate for 5 minutes so the coating sets.

4. Preheat the air fryer to `190°C` / `375°F` for 3 minutes. Spray the basket lightly. Arrange chicken in a single layer with space between pieces. Spray the tops lightly with oil.

5. Air fry cutlets for 12–15 minutes (tenders 10–12), flipping halfway and spraying the second side. Cook until deep golden and the thickest part reaches `74°C` / `165°F`.

6. Rest 2 minutes. Serve with lemon wedges (and a dip if you want).
