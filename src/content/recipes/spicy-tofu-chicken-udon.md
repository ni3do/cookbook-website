## Ingredients

### Chicken

- `300g` chicken mince
- `2` garlic cloves, grated
- `1 inch` ginger, grated
- `1 tbsp` soy sauce
- `1 tbsp` oyster sauce
- `2 tsp` mirin

### Silken tofu sauce

- `330g` silken tofu
- `1 tbsp` tahini
- `1 tbsp` soy sauce
- `1 tbsp` miso
- `2 tsp` rice vinegar
- `1 tbsp` gochugaru

### Noodles, salad & garnish

- `350g` frozen udon noodles
- `1` cucumber
- `2` spring onions
- coriander, to taste
- sesame seeds, to finish

## Steps

1. Add the silken tofu, tahini, soy sauce, miso, rice vinegar, and gochugaru to a blender. Pulse until smooth, but do not overblend or the sauce may turn runny. Set aside.
2. Julienne the spring onions, separating the greens if you like. Pick the coriander leaves.
3. Slice the cucumber into thin rounds, stack them, then cut into strips. Toss with the spring onion greens and coriander.
4. Heat a pan over medium heat. Add the chicken mince and let it take on some colour before breaking it up.
5. Deglaze the pan with mirin, then add the garlic, ginger, soy sauce, and oyster sauce. Cook for a few more minutes until the chicken is cooked through and glossy.
6. Cook the udon according to the packet instructions, then drain.
7. Plate with udon on the bottom, tofu sauce poured over, chicken on one side, and cucumber salad on the other.
8. Finish with sesame seeds and serve immediately.
