## Sauce

- `60ml` soy sauce
- `2 tbsp` peanut butter
- `1 tbsp` rice vinegar
- `2 tbsp` honey
- `2` cloves garlic, minced
- `1 tbsp` sriracha
- `2 tsp` sesame oil

## Chicken

- `900g` boneless skinless chicken thighs
- `1 tbsp` mayonnaise
- `1 tbsp` sriracha
- `1 tbsp` rice vinegar
- `2 tsp` onion powder
- `2 tsp` garlic powder
- `1 tsp` salt

## Bowls

- `3` medium carrots
- `225g` broccoli
- `1` small onion
- `500g` cooked rice (about 3 cups)
- `1 tbsp` oil
- `2` green onions (optional)
- `35g` crushed peanuts (optional)

## Steps

1. **Rice:** Cook enough rice for about `500g` cooked (ideally a day ahead, or use frozen).

2. **Chicken:** Heat the oven to `220°C` / `425°F`. Stir together mayo, sriracha, rice vinegar, onion powder, garlic powder, and salt, then toss the thighs in it (marinate up to 2 hours if you have time).

3. Lay the thighs smooth side down on a lined baking sheet. Roast 10 minutes, then switch to the grill (broiler) for about 5 minutes to brown — keep an eye on them. Rest briefly and cut into bite-sized pieces.

4. **Sauce:** Whisk all sauce ingredients together until smooth.

5. **Veg prep:** Thinly slice the onion, cut the broccoli into small florets, peel the carrots into ribbons, and slice the green onion tops.

6. In a large pan over medium-high heat, add the broccoli with about `60ml` water and let it steam until the water evaporates.

7. Push the broccoli aside, add the oil to the centre, and fry the onion 1–2 minutes for colour. Add the carrots and cook another 1–2 minutes.

8. Add the rice, pour over the sauce, and stir to coat. Mix in the chicken, then season with salt, pepper, or more sriracha to taste.

9. **Plate:** Divide among 5 containers and top with green onions and crushed peanuts.
