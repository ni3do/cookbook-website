## Ingredients

- `500g` pasta (penne)
- `400ml` cream
- `80g` tomato paste (around 5 tbsp)
- `60g` Parmesan cheese, finely grated
- nutmeg
- salt
- pepper
- bunch of parsley, chopped finely (plus a couple of leaves for garnish)

## Steps

1. Cook the pasta according to package instructions.
2. Meanwhile, in a large pot, bring the cream to a boil (be careful, it boils over quickly).
3. Reduce the heat to low and whisk in the tomato paste. Cook for about 5–10 minutes, or until the cream thickens.
4. Whisk the Parmesan, nutmeg, salt and pepper into the cream sauce.
5. Drain the pasta, add the sauce and stir to coat.
6. Garnish with parsley and serve.
