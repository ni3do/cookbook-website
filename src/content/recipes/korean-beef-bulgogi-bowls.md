## Ingredients

### Bulgogi

- `500g` ground beef
- `1 tbsp` canola oil
- `1 tbsp` onion, finely grated
- `3 tbsp` red apple, finely grated
- `3` garlic cloves, finely grated
- `1 tsp` ginger, finely grated
- `2½ tbsp` soy sauce (light or all-purpose)
- `1 tbsp` toasted sesame oil
- `1 tbsp` mirin (optional)
- `1 tbsp` brown sugar
- `⅛ tsp` black pepper

### Drizzle Sauce

- `2 tbsp` soy sauce
- `1 tbsp` sesame oil
- `2 tsp` brown sugar
- `2 tsp` rice vinegar

### To Serve

- cooked white rice (medium or short grain preferred)
- `2 tsp` canola oil (for the spinach)
- 4 large handfuls baby spinach
- `1` large carrot, julienned
- `1` avocado, mashed
- `¼ cup` pickled ginger
- `1½ cups` baby romaine lettuce
- `1 tsp` white sesame seeds
- `1` green onion, sliced

## Steps

1. Combine the bulgogi sauce ingredients in one bowl and the drizzle sauce ingredients in another. Set both aside.

2. Heat `2 tsp` oil in a large non-stick pan over high heat. Add the spinach in batches, tossing until just wilted (about 1 minute). Transfer to a bowl.

3. Add `1 tbsp` oil to the same pan. Brown the beef over high heat, breaking it apart as it cooks. Once the meat is no longer raw, pour in the bulgogi sauce.

4. Simmer rapidly, stirring occasionally, until the sauce mostly evaporates. Keep cooking and stirring until the beef is lightly caramelized but still moist.

5. Build the bowls: rice on the bottom, beef on top. Arrange the spinach and carrot alongside, add a dollop of mashed avocado and the pickled ginger, and tuck the lettuce in vertically. Finish with sesame seeds and green onion.

6. Drizzle with the sauce just before serving, then mix everything together at the table.
