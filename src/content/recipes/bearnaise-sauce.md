## Infused Vinegar

- `1½ tbsp` dry white wine (not sweet, fruity, or woody)
- `1½ tbsp` white wine vinegar (champagne vinegar also works)
- `¼ tsp` black pepper, coarsely crushed
- `1` small eschallot / shallot, peeled and finely sliced (or `1 tbsp` very finely sliced onion)
- `2` sprigs fresh tarragon

## Clarified Butter

- `225g` unsalted butter, cut into cubes (yields ~`175g` / `¾ cup` clarified butter after separating milk solids)
- Or use `175g` / `¾ cup` ghee, melted until hot

## Sauce

- `3` egg yolks, fridge-cold to separate, then left ~15 min to take the chill off
- `¼ tsp` kosher / cooking salt
- `½ tbsp` tarragon leaves, finely chopped
- `½ tbsp` chervil, finely chopped (or parsley)
- Water, as needed to loosen

## Steps

1. **Infuse the vinegar:** Put wine, vinegar, pepper, eschallot, and tarragon sprigs in a small saucepan over medium-low heat. Simmer 2 minutes, then take off the heat and stand 5 minutes. Strain, pressing hard — you want about `1–1½ tbsp` liquid. Cool ~5 minutes. If you have too much, reduce briefly; if too little, top up with water.

2. **Quick clarified butter:** Microwave the butter in a heatproof jug until melted (short bursts so it doesn’t erupt). Rest ~30 seconds so milk solids settle. Pour off `175g` / `¾ cup` of the clear gold butter; discard the milky solids. Keep it hot. (Or melt ghee until hot.)

3. **Blitz the base:** In a tall, narrow jug that fits a stick blender to the bottom, add yolks, cooled infused vinegar, and salt. Blitz briefly to combine.

4. **Emulsify:** With the blender running on high, slowly drizzle in the hot clarified butter over about 1 minute. Blitz another 10 seconds, moving the stick up and down.

5. **Loosen:** Add `1 tbsp` water and blitz. Add more water `1 tsp` at a time until it oozes slowly over a steak (thick, but not mayo-stiff).

6. **Finish:** Stir in the chopped tarragon and chervil. Serve warm (thermos keeps it ~1 hour). Makes enough for ~3 generous steaks or 4 sensible portions.
