## Ingredients

- `60ml` dry white wine
- `60ml` white wine vinegar
- `1` small shallot, peeled and thinly sliced
- `1 tbsp` tarragon leaves
- `3` egg yolks
- `1 tsp` lemon juice
- `¼ tsp` kosher salt
- `115g` unsalted butter or ghee, melted and hot (add more for a thinner consistency)
- `2 tsp` finely chopped tarragon leaves

## Steps

1. Add the wine, vinegar, shallot, and tarragon to a small saucepan over medium-high heat. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a simmer until the liquid has reduced to about 1½ tablespoons, 5 to 7 minutes.
2. Strain through a fine-mesh sieve, pressing with the back of a spoon to extract as much flavour as possible. Let cool for 5 minutes.
3. Melt the butter until hot; cover it if using a microwave to avoid splattering.
4. Add the egg yolks, infused vinegar, lemon juice, and salt to a high-powered blender. Blend for 5 seconds, until combined.
5. With the blender running on medium-high, slowly stream the hot butter through the lid opening until emulsified.
6. Pour into a small bowl, stir in the finely chopped tarragon, and serve warm.
