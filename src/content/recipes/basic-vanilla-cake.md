## Ingredients

- `200g` unsalted butter
- `200g` caster sugar
- `8g` vanilla sugar
- 4 medium eggs
- `200g` self-raising flour
- pinch of salt

## Steps

1. In a bowl, combine the butter, sugar, and vanilla sugar. Beat with a mixer until creamy and pale. Add the eggs one by one, mixing well after each addition until the batter is smooth.
2. Add the self-raising flour and salt to the batter. Continue mixing until all the dry ingredients are fully incorporated.
3. Pour the batter into a greased and floured loaf or cake tin. Bake for 60 minutes at 175°C (conventional top and bottom heat).
4. Check for doneness by inserting a wooden skewer or cake tester into the centre. If it comes out clean, the cake is ready.
5. Let the cake cool in the tin for a few minutes, then turn it out onto a wire rack and allow it to cool completely before slicing.
