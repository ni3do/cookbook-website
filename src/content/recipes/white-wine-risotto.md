## Ingredients

- `320g` arborio or carnaroli rice
- `1` small onion or 2 shallots, finely minced
- `2 tbsp` olive oil
- `30g` butter (for cooking)
- `160ml` dry white wine
- `1.1L` vegetable or light chicken stock, kept hot
- `90g` Parmesan, finely grated
- `40g` cold butter (to finish)
- Salt and black pepper to taste

## Optional Finish

- Lemon zest
- Fresh parsley, chopped

## Steps

1. Keep the stock in a saucepan at a bare simmer. Taste and season it lightly — the rice will take on that seasoning.

2. In a wide, heavy pan over medium-low heat, warm the olive oil and `30g` butter. Add the onion/shallot with a pinch of salt and sweat until soft and translucent, about 5–7 minutes. Do not brown.

3. Add the rice and stir for 1–2 minutes until the grains look slightly translucent at the edges (toasted, not colored).

4. Pour in the white wine. Stir until it is mostly absorbed and the alcohol smell has cooked off.

5. Add a ladle of hot stock. Stir often, letting it absorb before adding the next ladle. Continue for about 18–20 minutes until the rice is creamy and al dente. You may not need all the stock; stop when the texture is right.

6. Take the pan off the heat. Rest 1 minute, then beat in the cold butter and Parmesan until glossy and loose. Loosen with a splash of stock if it seizes.

7. Season with salt and pepper. Finish with lemon zest or parsley if you like. Serve immediately.
